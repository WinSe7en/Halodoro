# Halodoro Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Halodoro is a focus timer for the Mac menu bar. It does not collect, send or share any information about you.

## What the app stores

Halodoro keeps the following on your Mac only, inside the app's own sandboxed folder:

- **Your settings**, such as sounds, colors and Halo style.
- **Your history**, meaning the focus blocks and breaks you ran, with their times and lengths, used for the dots on the clock card.
- **A local log**, written by macOS's standard system log, used only if you choose to collect diagnostics for a bug report.

None of this leaves your Mac. The developer cannot see it.

## What the app does not do

- It makes no network connections.
- It has no account, sign-in or sign-up.
- It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code that collects data.
- It does not read your contacts, calendar, location, photos, microphone, camera or files.

## Sharing

Because nothing is collected, nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone.

## Deleting your data

Quit Halodoro and delete the app. To remove its stored settings and history as well, delete its folder in `~/Library/Containers/`.

## Diagnostics you send yourself

If you report a bug, you may choose to attach a diagnostics folder made by the app's collection script. It contains window positions, timer states and a few settings, so look through it first. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

## Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a future version adds syncing, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
